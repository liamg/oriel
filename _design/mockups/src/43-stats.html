How it's measured· the worker reads each provider's own figures: Claude's /usage windows through the Oriel mod ($.session.usage()), Codex's account/rateLimits/read · nothing is prompted, so measuring spends no tokens · burn is the slope over the last 30 min (5h) or 24h (weekly)
The pages
Session stats is a main tab for one session: totals, cost over its life by token kind with idle gaps and turns marked, cost per repo, models, tools with errors and timing, hours, effort.
Global stats is a main page across every session and worker: a range, a group-by, filters, totals against the previous period, the headline chart, a table of groups and four small breakdowns.
Usage is a main page per provider: each plan window as a meter with its reset, burn rate and projection; 7 days of history with the resets; and which repos burned the window.
Every cost is est.: tokens are counted, cost is tokens × the price table, so a price change re-costs history. Usage's percentages are the provider's own.
Grouped by repo
Repo is the default group, not the session or its working directory. Repos keep their project colour from the sidebar (42) in every chart, on Usage too.
A multi-repo session splits by its share of edits and file tool calls per repo; requests that touch no file follow the same split. Totals stay exact.
Split off (global) or count whole session (session tab): each repo is charged the full session, the table says the rows overlap, and the total stays the real one.
Burned the window spreads a window's points over repos by their share of its tokens; use outside Oriel (claude.ai, another machine) is its own row.
Where it comes from
The worker scans transcripts incrementally: per request, tokens by kind, model, effort, time; per tool call, name, error, duration. It keeps an offset per file, so a rescan reads only what's new.
Remote workers report their own rollups over the bus; the page merges them. A worker that's offline shows its last report, marked stale.
Plan windows are the provider's own: Claude's /usage through the Oriel mod, Codex's rate limits. No prompt is sent, so measuring costs nothing. With no session open, the resident utility session reports.
Burn is the slope over the last 30 min (5h) or 24h (weekly); the projection extends it to the reset. Active time counts gaps under 5 min.
Keys and entry points
Sidebar: Stats, Usage and Jobs sit beside Settings below a second divider, each with a figure; icons pinned to the foot in the narrow form. Settings stays last.
Title bar meters open Usage, with resets and burn on hover. The tally's $ opens Session stats. The start view has a link to each.
:Stats :Usage :Jobs; :Stats repo=api 7d opens filtered, :Stats session this session. Proposed: ⌘⇧S ⌘⇧U ⌘⇧J (⌘⇧S is often "save all"; nothing in 42 uses it).
In Stats: g then r s k m p d groups; [ ] range; ⏎ drills in, ⌫ out. In Usage: r rereads. esc closes. Charts are one widget.Chart.
