Weekly usage window

The WK tat in the titlebar · Claude Max · provider: claude
Right now

Amber above 70%, red above 90%. Hatched band is the projection.

What it means

Your plan allows a fixed amount of model use per rolling week. This is how much of it every session on every worker has used together — not just this one. When it hits 100%, sessions on this account pause until the window resets.

The projection extrapolates the last 24 h. It's a hint, not a promise: one long Opus session moves it a lot.
