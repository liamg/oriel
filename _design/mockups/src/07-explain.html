Step 03/07

Invoices round each line once, and remember how

With the rounding function settled, the next question is where it's called. The answer is exactly one place: NewLine. Totals are sums of already-rounded lines, which is what keeps the invoice total and the line items in agreement. The stored Rounding field is what lets Regenerate tell new lines from historical ones.
