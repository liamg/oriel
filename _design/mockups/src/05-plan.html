Round invoice lines half-to-even

Goal · Approach · Pieces · Risks · Out of scope

Goal

Invoice totals must equal the sum of their rounded lines. Today they drift by up to one minor unit on tiered plans.

Approach

  • Add a RoundingMode type in internal/pricing.
  • Round each line in minor units, ties to even.
  • Totals sum the rounded lines; never re-round a total.
● agent edited 4s ago

Currency precision comes from a hard-coded table in round.go currency.go, keyed by ISO 4217 code, so JPY (0) and BHD (3) are handled.

Pieces

mermaid · flowchart

Risks

  • Historical invoices must not change: only new invoices round this way.
  • Partners reconcile against our CSV export; flag the change in the changelog.
