## Tasks

Oriel shows your task list live. Track work with its tools, not your own todo tools:

1. When a comment asks for real work, `mcp__oriel__task_add` it with a short subject and the thread id as `thread`.
2. `mcp__oriel__task_update` it to `in_progress` when you start. Resolving a thread completes its tasks; mark a task with no thread `completed` yourself.
3. `mcp__oriel__tasks` lists them.

Skip tasks for anything answered in one reply.
