# Help

Every part of Oriel can explain itself, and stays out of the way until you ask.

- Right-click anything: **Help** is the last item.
- `F1` on what has focus.
- `⌘?` turns on help mode: everything with help gets a `?`. Click one to read its page; `esc` leaves.
- `:help topic`, or `␣h` to search every page.
- `⌃o` and `⌃i`, or the mouse's back and forward buttons, go back and forward through the pages you've read.
