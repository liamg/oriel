# Panes and docks

Every view lives in the layout: splits and tabs in the middle, docks along the left, right and bottom, floats,
and a drop-down.

## Tabs {#tabs}

A pane can hold several views as tabs. Click one to show it; drag it elsewhere to move it.

## Splits {#splits}

Drag the line between two panes to resize them. `⌃W =` evens them out.

## Drag and drop {#drag-and-drop}

Drag a tab onto another pane's edge to split it, onto its middle to join its tabs, or onto a window edge to dock it.
